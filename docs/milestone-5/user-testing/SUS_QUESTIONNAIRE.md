### Moderator Scoring Worksheet (Brooke, 1996)

1. For odd-numbered items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9): Score contribution = $\text{User Response} - 1$.
2. For even-numbered items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10): Score contribution = $5 - \text{User Response}$.
3. Sum the 10 score contributions: $\text{Sum} = \sum_{i=1}^{10} \text{Contribution}_i$.
4. Multiply the sum by **2.5** to calculate the final composite SUS Score (0 to 100).

$$\mathbf{Composite\ SUS\ Score} = \text{Sum} \times 2.5 = \underline{\hspace{2cm}}\ / 100$$
