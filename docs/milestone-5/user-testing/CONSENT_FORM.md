### Participant Briefing
You are invited to participate in a usability research session evaluating **DIME**, an AI-powered product intelligence and idea validation platform. The objective of this study is to evaluate the clarity, usability, visual ergonomics, and perceived value of DIME's idea submission and market intelligence workflows.

### What You Will Do
- You will be asked to perform 9 realistic tasks using the DIME web application (e.g., creating an idea, exploring market competitors, reviewing technical feasibility, and exporting validation reports).
- You are encouraged to "think aloud" as you navigate, sharing what you observe, what is clear, and what is confusing.
- The session is expected to take approximately **30 to 45 minutes**.
- Following task completion, you will complete a brief 10-question System Usability Scale (SUS) questionnaire and a short debrief interview.

### Confidentiality & Privacy
- Your identity will be kept completely confidential. You will be assigned an anonymous participant code (e.g., `P01`, `P02`).
- No personal passwords, confidential business secrets, or financial data will be recorded.
- Collected data (time on task, completion status, usability feedback, SUS scores) will be utilized solely for academic research and software improvement.

### Voluntary Participation
- Your participation is entirely voluntary.
- You have the right to skip any question or stop the session at any time without penalty or explanation.
