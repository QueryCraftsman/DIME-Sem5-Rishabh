**Instructions:** Please read each task carefully. Perform the task on the computer at your own pace. Speak your thoughts aloud as you work through each task. You may ask the session moderator questions if you encounter technical blockers.

### Task 1: Access the Application & Dashboard
*Scenario:* You are a founder exploring early-stage concepts. You want to access your workspace.
- **Instruction:** Log in to the application and navigate to the Executive Dashboard.
- **Completion Check:** Confirm that you can see your personal welcome greeting and the 5 KPI metric cards.

---

### Task 2: Submit a New Product Idea
*Scenario:* You have conceived a new product concept: an AI-guided educational tool or developer infrastructure utility.
- **Instruction:** Navigate to "New Analysis" and use the Guided Form to submit your concept. Provide a title, description, target audience, and domain. Configure basic parameters (budget, timeline, and team size).
- **Completion Check:** Confirm that the idea is saved and validation is triggered.

---

### Task 3: Monitor Live Validation Progress
*Scenario:* DIME is analyzing the market and academic literature for your idea.
- **Instruction:** Watch the live progress indicator.
- **Completion Check:** Identify which analytical stage DIME is currently performing (e.g., Query Planning, Evidence Retrieval, or Scoring).

---

### Task 4: Explore Market Intelligence & Competitors
*Scenario:* You want to understand who else is solving this problem.
- **Instruction:** Open the completed analysis and navigate to the "Market Intelligence" tab. Locate the Competitor Matrix.
- **Completion Check:** Name at least two competitors discovered by DIME and explain whether they are direct or indirect alternatives.

---

### Task 5: Evaluate Technical Feasibility
*Scenario:* You need to determine whether building an MVP is realistic for your team.
- **Instruction:** Navigate to the "Technical Feasibility" tab. Review the Feasibility Score and the Recommended Architecture.
- **Completion Check:** Identify the primary engineering challenge or bottleneck highlighted by DIME.

---

### Task 6: Review Risks & Strategic Recommendations
*Scenario:* You want to know what could go wrong and what you should do next.
- **Instruction:** Navigate to the "Risks & Recommendations" tab.
- **Completion Check:** Identify the highest-priority risk and read the concrete mitigation step suggested by the system.

---

### Task 7: Inspect Supporting Evidence
*Scenario:* An investor asks where your market demand data originated.
- **Instruction:** Navigate to the "Evidence Explorer" tab. Locate an evidence snippet supporting your analysis.
- **Completion Check:** Identify the source title, source type (academic paper, GitHub repo, or web article), and click its link to verify authenticity.

---

### Task 8: Preview & Export Executive Summary Report
*Scenario:* You want to share a PDF or CSV report of this validation with your team.
- **Instruction:** Navigate to the "Report" tab and click the Export button.
- **Completion Check:** Verify that the exported file downloads to your browser and opens cleanly.

---

### Task 9: Overall Debrief & System Usability Scale
- **Instruction:** Complete the 10-question SUS questionnaire on the provided sheet and share your overall reflections with the moderator.
